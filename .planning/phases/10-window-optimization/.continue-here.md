## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| WindowOptions override | `WindowOptions(size: 960x540)` in `waitUntilReadyToShow` may override manual setPosition/setSize | advisory | Set geometry INSIDE callback AFTER removeBorder, or use saved size in WindowOptions |
| ensureVisible timing | `ensureVisible()` before `show()` may detect wrong monitor | advisory | Call after `show()` + `focus()` |
| _savedFrame crash loss | `_savedFrame` is in-memory only; crash during fullscreen loses exit geometry | advisory | Do NOT auto-restore fullscreen; clear isFullscreen on startup |
