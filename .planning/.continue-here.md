## Critical Insight (Why This Works)

window_manager internally uses `WM_NCCALCSIZE` to hide the title bar while keeping `WS_CAPTION` and `WS_THICKFRAME` intact. Our custom C++ approach removed `WS_CAPTION` which broke DWM animation pipeline. This is the root cause fix.
